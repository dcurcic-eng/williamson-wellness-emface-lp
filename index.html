What is EMFACE

One 20-minute session. Two layers of lift.

EMFACE is a needle-free facial treatment by BTL. It works on the muscle that holds your brows and cheeks up and on the skin that sits over it, at the same time. A study in the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology linked it to measurable soft-tissue lift.

Radiofrequency

Gently heats the skin to wake up collagen and elastin, smoothing fine lines and softening texture.

HIFES

High-intensity facial electrical stimulation contracts the small muscles that support your brows, cheeks and jawline.

Made for

  • Forehead lines
  • Brow heaviness
  • Flattened cheeks
  • Early jowls
  • Fine lines
  • Mild skin laxity

How much does EMFACE cost?

Pricing is personalized and quoted at your consultation, usually as a series of four. It is self-pay and not covered by insurance. Book through this page to save 20% on your series.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe warmth and a tapping, contracting sensation. Your provider adjusts the intensity to keep you comfortable, and no numbing cream is needed.

How long is a session?

The treatment itself takes about 20 minutes while you lie back and relax. Plan on 30 to 40 minutes for the whole visit, then head straight back to work or errands.

When will I see results?

Many clients notice a fresher, more lifted look after the second or third session. Results keep building for several weeks after your final visit, with the fullest change around three months later. Results vary from person to person.

How many sessions do I need?

Most clients complete four sessions spaced about a week apart. Nancy confirms the right number at your consultation, and many people add periodic maintenance visits to keep results going.

Is EMFACE like Botox?

They work in opposite ways. Neurotoxins relax muscles to soften expression lines, while EMFACE strengthens the muscles that support the face and stimulates collagen, with no injections. We do not offer injectables, but we will explain the differences honestly.

Am I a candidate?

Good candidates are adults who want to soften fine lines, lift the brow or cheeks, or firm mild laxity without needles or surgery. People with pacemakers, metal implants in the face or who are pregnant should not be treated.

Is there any downtime?

No. Some clients see mild redness or warmth that usually settles within an hour. You can wear makeup, go back to work and follow your normal routine the same day.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes. EMFACE pairs well with Exion Face for needle-free firming and RF microneedling for texture and pores. Nancy can sequence these so each one supports the others, based on your skin and your schedule.
